## 1. These terms

These terms govern your use of LMX's delivery services and the LMX client portal.
By requesting an account you agree to them on behalf of your business. If you do
not have authority to agree on its behalf, do not request an account.

## 2. Requesting an account

Requesting an account is an application, not an agreement by us to carry your
deliveries. We review each application and may approve or decline it at our
discretion, without giving reasons. You cannot place orders until your account is
approved, and approval may be withdrawn.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you give us, and for the
security of the logins created under your account. You must tell us promptly if a
login should no longer have access.

## 3. What LMX does

LMX is a delivery operator. We carry your goods using our own drivers and
vehicles, or capacity we arrange. We are not a software vendor, a broker, or a
marketplace. Nothing in these terms licenses software to you: the portal is how
you send us work, not a product you are buying, and we charge for deliveries
rather than for access to it.

## 4. Orders, collection and delivery

When you place an order you tell us where to collect from, where it goes, and how
urgent it is. We respond with a **collection commitment**: a time by which we
undertake to collect the goods.

Any delivery time we show you is an **estimate**. It is our best expectation based
on distance and expected travel conditions, it is not a commitment, and it is
labelled as an estimate wherever it appears.

You are responsible for packaging goods adequately for transport and for
describing them accurately. We may decline, hold, or return an order that is
unsafe, unlawful, improperly packaged, mis-described, or outside the area we
serve.

Some deliveries require proof of delivery — photographs, a signature, or a
recipient code — and what is required is set per order. Where a recipient is
unavailable or refuses a delivery, we will follow the exception process we have
agreed with you.

## 5. Prices and payment

Prices are agreed with you when your account is approved and are charged per
delivery according to the urgency tier of each order, together with any distance,
piece, weight or minimum-charge components we have agreed. Each invoice shows how
every charge was calculated.

We may change prices on notice. Payment terms are as stated on your invoices.

Where we collect payment from your customer at the door on your behalf, we collect
the amount you have specified and remit it to you. We are not a party to your
transaction with your customer.

## 6. Service levels and credits

Where we miss a collection commitment we credit your account against that
delivery, at the rate set out in your service schedule, without you having to ask.
Credits appear as itemised lines on your invoice. Credits are the remedy for a
missed commitment, and they are calculated from our own records of when collection
and delivery actually happened.

## 7. Your customers' information

To deliver for you we necessarily hold information about your customers: names,
delivery addresses, contact numbers, and any delivery notes you send us. We hold
it on your behalf, we use it to carry out your deliveries and to operate and
improve our service, and we do not sell it.

Where we send a tracking link to a recipient, it identifies you as the sender.

How long we keep this information, and how someone asks for a copy of it or for it
to be deleted, is set out in our privacy policy, which forms part of these terms.

## 8. Operational data

We record how deliveries actually happen: times, locations, routes, distances,
vehicle and driver activity, exceptions, proof of delivery, and outcomes. This
operational record is ours.

We use it to run and improve our dispatch and routing systems. That includes
analysis and model training across all of the work we carry, not only yours.

We do this on data describing *how a delivery was performed*. Where we use it
beyond the operation of your own account, it is aggregated and de-identified so
that it cannot reasonably be traced back to you, to your customers, or to your
commercial arrangements with us. We do not use your prices, your customer lists,
or your order volumes to the benefit of a competitor of yours.

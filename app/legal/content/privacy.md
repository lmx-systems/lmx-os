This policy explains what personal information LMX holds, why we hold it, how long
we keep it, and how to ask us about it. It covers three groups of people, who reach
us in quite different ways:

- **People at the businesses we deliver for** — the person who signs up, and their
  colleagues who send us orders.
- **People receiving a delivery** — our customer's customer. We hold your details
  because the business sending you something gave them to us.
- **Drivers** — people who carry deliveries for us.

If you are receiving a delivery and want your information changed or removed, the
business sending it to you is usually the right first contact, because they decide
what to send us. You can also come to us directly using the details at the end.

## 1. Businesses we deliver for

**What we hold.** Your company name, the area you deliver in, and for each person
using the portal: their name, email address, phone number if given, and a stored
form of their password that cannot be reversed. We also hold which version of our
terms was accepted and when. For applications we decline, we hold the application
itself.

**Why.** To decide whether to take you on, to let your people sign in, to accept
and price your orders, to invoice you, and to contact you about the service.

**How long.** While your account is open, and then for seven years, because
delivery and billing records are business records we are expected to be able to
produce. Declined applications are kept for twelve months and then deleted.

## 2. People receiving a delivery

**What we hold.** Your name, the delivery address, your phone number if the sender
gave us one, and any delivery instructions they included. When a delivery is made
we hold the time, the location, and the proof of delivery — which may be
photographs of the delivered goods at your door and a signature, depending on what
the sender asked for. If you paid at the door, we hold that a payment was collected
and how.

**Why.** To carry out the delivery the sender asked us to make, to prove that we
made it, and to tell you it is coming.

**We hold this on the sender's behalf.** They chose to send it to us. We do not use
it to market anything to you, we do not sell it, and we do not build a profile of
you across the businesses that use us.

**Tracking links.** If you get a text with a tracking link, that link shows the
status of your delivery, an estimated time, and — only while your delivery is the
next one on the driver's route — the vehicle's position. The link stops working
shortly after your delivery is completed.

**How long.** Delivery records, including your name and address, are kept for seven
years as part of the business record of the delivery. Photographs and signatures
taken as proof of delivery are kept for two years and then deleted.

## 3. Drivers

**What we hold.** Your name, phone number, and email address. Your vehicle and
plate. Your driving licence and insurance documents, which we hold as images and
which are checked by a person before you can go on shift. Your shifts, the
deliveries you made, the proof you captured, exceptions you reported, and payments
made to you.

**Location.** While you are on duty, the app records your position at regular
intervals so that we can dispatch work sensibly, give recipients an accurate
arrival time, and evidence what happened on a delivery. **We do not record your
location when you are off duty.**

**Why.** To verify you are entitled to drive and insured, to route and dispatch
work, to pay you, and to resolve disputes about a delivery.

**How long.** Your location history is kept for ninety days and then deleted
automatically. Your licence and insurance documents are kept while you drive for us
and then for four years. Delivery and payment records are kept for seven years.

## 4. Text messages and calls

We send text messages for sign-in codes, tracking links, pickup notifications, and
payment problems, and we keep a record of the messages we sent and to which number.
Where a call is connected through our system we record that it happened, between
which numbers, and for how long. **We do not record the content of calls.** These
records are kept for two years.

## 5. Addresses and mapping

We convert addresses into map coordinates so that we can route to them, and we
store the result so that the same address does not have to be looked up again. That
store is keyed on the address itself and is not linked to a person.

## 6. Who else sees it

We use other companies to run the service. Each sees only what it needs:

- **Cloud hosting and databases** — where the service runs and data is stored.
- **A text-messaging provider** — to deliver messages, which means it processes the
  recipient's number and the message.
- **A mapping and routing provider** — to convert addresses to coordinates and to
  plan routes, which means it processes addresses.
- **A payroll and payments provider** — to pay drivers, which means it processes
  driver identity and bank details.
- **An email provider** — to send account email.
- **File storage** — for proof-of-delivery images and driver documents.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's
advertising. We may disclose information where the law requires it, to protect
someone's safety, or to a professional adviser or insurer in connection with a
claim.

## 7. Security

Access to the system requires an account, and access is checked on every request.
Passwords are stored in a form that cannot be reversed. Proof-of-delivery images
and driver documents are held in separate storage with separate access rules,
because a photograph of a parcel and a photograph of a driving licence are not the
same kind of thing. Traffic is encrypted in transit.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to
correct it, or ask us to delete it. Where we hold information on behalf of a
business that sends us deliveries, we will usually need to refer your request to
them, and we will tell you when we do.

Where we are legally required to keep a record — a delivery we performed, an
invoice we issued, a payment we made — we will keep that record even after a
deletion request, and we will tell you what we have kept and why.

## 9. Cookies

The portal sets what it needs to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising
cookies and we do not track you across other websites.

## 10. Children

The service is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information about
children.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We record which version is current and when it took
effect, and where a change is material we will tell the businesses we deliver for
before it takes effect.

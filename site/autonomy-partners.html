For autonomy partners

The industrial demand you cannot aggregate yourself.

You built the vehicle. We run the operation it needs to plug into.

The problem from your side

It is not a technology limit. It is an aggregation limit.

Industrial last mile is thousands of independent distributors, each with modest volume, no shared infrastructure, and no way to integrate with you. Going direct means a separate commercial relationship, a separate integration, and a separate depot for every one of them. The unit economics never close.

That is why the autonomy programs scaling today are consumer programs.

We want the drone service layer on top of an existing, aggregated supply network.
Chief executive, autonomous delivery operator

Where we actually are

Human-driven vehicles today, built multi-modal from the ground up.

Direct about status, because you will find out anyway.

LMX operates human-driven vehicles today. Our system is built multi-modality from the ground up — every order carries the data needed to evaluate whether a machine could carry it, and we evaluate that on live deliveries whether or not one is available.

Autonomous capacity scales into the network as service availability and cost clear the threshold where it improves the operation rather than complicating it. We are not waiting for that to build the layer underneath it.

Step 01
Order enters with weight, dimensions, window, destination class
Step 02
Eligibility check against every vehicle class in the network
Step 03
Machine if available and economic, human-driven vehicle otherwise
Step 04
Fallback timer runs; clean release swaps to a human-driven vehicle automatically
Outcome
The same delivery promise, whichever carried it
